# 🔪 Word Imposter

> **Everyone gets a word. One player gets a different one. Can you uncover the Imposter before it's too late?**

Word Imposter is a fast-paced **real-time multiplayer party game** where deception, deduction, and creativity collide. Gather your friends, join the same room, give clever one-word clues, and figure out who doesn't quite belong.

Whether you're playing with friends on Discord, during a game night, or just looking for a quick challenge, every round is different—and every clue matters.

## ✨ How to Play

1. **Create** a room or **join** one using an invite link or room code.
2. Every player secretly receives a word.
3. One random player is the **Imposter** and receives a similar—but different—word.
4. Players take turns giving **one-word clues** without revealing their word.
5. After each round, decide whether to continue or vote.
6. Find the Imposter before they blend in and fool everyone!
